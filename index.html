Our 7-Stage Recruitment Process

01

Manpower Request

Inform us about your manpower requirements via email or request.

02

Demand Letter

Provide legal documentation (Demand Letter & Power of Attorney).

03

Permission

Apply for and receive government bureau/protectorate permission.

04

Advertisement

Targeted campaigns across print media, social networks, and our active database.

05

Recruitment

Client delegation interviews, live online video interviews, or trade testing.

06

Visa Stamping

Medical examinations, visa endorsement, and legal documentation clearance.

07

Deployment

Air-ticket arrangement, comprehensive pre-departure briefing, and handover.
